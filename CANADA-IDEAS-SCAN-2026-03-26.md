## Ideas Ranked by Feasibility + Moat + Demand

### Tier 1: High Confidence

#### 1. **Canadian Freelancer Tax Tracker (CRA T776/T4A)**
- **Problem:** 3M+ Canadian freelancers manually track expenses, fill T776 forms. No dedicated tool exists.
- **Moat:** CRA T776 + T4A auto-import, bilingual (EN/FR Quebec requirement), Interac settlement tracking.
- **MVP:** Bank API connection (Plaid), expense categorization, T776 PDF export. 4-6 weeks.
- **Revenue:** Freemium (basic free) + Pro ($9.99/mo). Target: 5k users @ 20% = $10k/mo.
- **Score:** 8.5/10 — Obvious demand, defensible moat, Joe's freelancer audience.

#### 2. **Canadian Grant/Tax Credit Finder**
- **Problem:** Thousands of Canadian grants/credits exist. Businesses miss $5k-50k/year annually.
- **Moat:** Bilingual + Canadian focus, hard to replicate for US competitors.
- **MVP:** Database of ~500 grants. Quiz-based matching ("Province? Revenue? Industry?"). 4-5 weeks.
- **Revenue:** Freemium + Pro ($7.99/mo) + B2B accountant white-label ($49/mo).
- **Score:** 8/10 — Easy build, obvious demand, defensible.

#### 3. **Canadian Bill Pay + Recurring Expense Automation**
- **Problem:** Canadians pay 15+ bills manually. No good tool for SMB/personal combined.
- **Moat:** Interac e-Transfer settlement, Canadian utility/telecom knowledge, bilingual.
- **MVP:** Bill calendar, reminders, one-click Interac pay, CSV export for taxes. 6-8 weeks.
- **Revenue:** Freemium (5 bills free) + Pro ($4.99/mo) + B2B SMB tier ($19.99/mo).
- **Score:** 7/10 — Good market, moderate build. BUT overlaps Even Us Up; improve that first (0-20 visitor problem).

#### 4. **Canadian Stock Dividend Tracker + RRSP Optimization**
- **Problem:** Self-directed RRSP/TFSA investors manually track dividends, tax optimization is complex.
- **Market:** 3M Canadian self-directed investors. TAM: $50M+.
- **MVP:** Portfolio import (Plaid/CSV), dividend calculation, TFSA vs RRSP allocation, tax summary. 6-7 weeks.
- **Revenue:** Freemium (basic tracking) + Pro ($9.99/mo — tax planning, rebalance alerts).
- **Score:** 7.5/10 — Good market, but Wealthsimple competes. Moderate defensibility.

#### 5. **Bilingual Content Creator Toolkit (EN/FR Auto-Translation)**
- **Problem:** Canadian creators want both EN/FR audiences (10M Francophones). Manual translation costs $500-2k/mo.
- **Moat:** Quebec dialect + French Canadian culture. Better than generic AI.
- **MVP:** Paste EN → AI translate to FR (Claude API) → human editor. Export SRT/TXT/MP3. 3-4 weeks.
- **Revenue:** Usage-based ($0.10/1k words) + Pro ($19.99/mo unlimited). Target: 500 creators @ 20% = $2k/mo.
- **Score:** 6.5/10 — Niche demand, low build. AI translation commoditizing (risk).
