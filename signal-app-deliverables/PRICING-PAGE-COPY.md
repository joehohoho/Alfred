## FAQ Section

### "Is this really 55% accurate?"
"Yes. We define 'accurate' as: signal direction (long/short) + price range hit within 7 days. We publicly show this on our accuracy dashboard, updated daily. On weeks we underperform (like last month during flash crashes), we say so."

### "How are your signals different from [Competitor]?"
"Three things: (1) Transparency — we show real accuracy, not estimated claims. (2) Speed — real-time alerts vs. delayed notifications. (3) Cost — $19.99/mo vs. their $50/mo. Try free tier for 2 weeks. If signals don't match the accuracy dashboard, upgrade to Pro with confidence."

### "What if I don't have a crypto exchange account yet?"
"No problem. Free tier shows signals with 48h delay (for learning). When you open an exchange account (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.), upgrade to Pro for real-time signals. We have guides for all major exchanges."

### "Can I see a signal before paying?"
"Absolutely. Free tier gives you 3-5 signals per week. See the quality yourself. No hidden upsells."

### "What's the difference between Pro and Professional?"
"Pro is for active traders (real-time signals, backtesting). Professional (Sept 2026) adds API access, custom filters, and bot integration for traders building automated systems. If you don't code, Pro is for you."

### "Can I cancel anytime?"
"Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel in settings anytime. Keep access until the end of your billing cycle."

### "Do you have a money-back guarantee?"
"Better — try free tier first. If you don't see signal quality in the first week, upgrade to Pro risk-free for 7 days (money back guarantee). See signals work in real-time before committing."

### "How often are signals sent?"
"Pro tier: 25+ per week (avg 3-5 per day). Depends on market opportunities. Quiet market = fewer signals. Bull run = more signals. You control notification frequency in settings."

## CTA (Call-to-Action) Strategy

### Primary CTA: "Get Started Free"
**Button placement:** Hero (above fold)  
**Button color:** Green (growth/money)  
**Destination:** Sign-up flow (email + password)  
**Follow-up:** Email welcome sequence (see EMAIL-SEQUENCES.md)

### Secondary CTA: "Upgrade to Pro"
**Button placement:** On Free tier card  
**Trigger:** User views free tier for >5 seconds  
**Destination:** Stripe checkout (pre-select Pro tier)  
**Copy variant:** "See Pro signals in action"

### Tertiary CTA: "Join Waitlist" (Professional)
**Button placement:** Professional tier card  
**Destination:** Email capture (waitlist)  
**Follow-up:** Email notification when Professional tier launches (Sept 2026)

## Design Notes (for frontend engineer)

1. **Pricing table** should be sticky (follow as user scrolls) on mobile
2. **Feature comparison matrix** should collapse to accordion on mobile (tap to expand)
3. **Accuracy dashboard preview** should be interactive (hover to see live data)
4. **CTA buttons** should change color on hover (green → darker green)
5. **Testimonials** should auto-rotate (3 testimonials, cycle every 5 seconds)
6. **FAQ** should have search (filter questions as user types)
7. **Trust badges** (SSL lock, encrypted, etc.) in footer
