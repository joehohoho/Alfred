## COPY GUIDELINES (FOR TEAM)

✅ **DO:**
- Use "you" (speak to 1 person, not 1,000)
- Show specific numbers (55% accuracy, not "better than average")
- Admit failures (we're not hiding our losses)
- Make CTAs clear (one main button, one main ask)
- Keep subject lines under 60 chars
- Personalize (use first name, signal results)

- Use hype ("Revolutionary!", "Game-changer!")
- Make false claims ("100% win rate" — you'll lose trust)
- Use "we" too much (focus on user benefit, not company news)
- Bury the CTA (make it easy to find)
- Send more than 3 emails per week
- Use all-caps (too aggressive)
