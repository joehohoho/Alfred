## Description
Research summary for Joe's early-stage Stock/Crypto Buy/Sell Signal App.

Recommendation:
Start with freemium + a single paid Pro tier, not one-time purchase and not B2B-first.

Suggested pricing:
- Free: delayed signals, limited watchlist, basic alerts, weekly digest, social proof/results snapshots
- Pro: C$19 to C$29/month, target launch price C$24/month or C$240/year
- Founding-member offer: C$15/month lifetime while active for first 25 to 50 users
- Optional later tier: C$49 to C$79/month only after clear premium differentiation like SMS/Discord realtime alerts, broker/API integration, backtests, model explanations, portfolio/risk overlays

Why this fits Joe's audience:
- Early-stage retail users are price sensitive and skeptical of signal quality claims
- Lower-friction subscription beats a one-time fee because signals are ongoing value, not static software
- Freemium helps trust-building, list growth, and proof collection before heavier CAC spend
- B2B API licensing is attractive later, but too early as the primary model because it needs stable performance, documentation, SLA expectations, and outbound sales effort

Competitor benchmarks:
1. TradingView: freemium ladder. Paid tiers roughly C$19.95/mo Essential, C$41.95/mo Plus, C$82.95/mo Premium. Strong proof that traders accept subscriptions when alerts and workflow depth increase.
2. Cryptohopper: free plus paid ladder. Public pricing currently shows Explorer about $24.16/mo, Adventurer $57.50/mo, Hero $107.50/mo. Good example of upselling automation/power features after free entry.
3. TrendSpider: premium-first, much higher pricing. Public pricing/schema shows Standard around $89/mo and Premium around $149/mo. Shows advanced tooling can command high ARPU, but that level is likely too expensive for Joe's early audience unless the app becomes deeply differentiated.

Monetization model ranking:
1. Best now: freemium + one core subscription tier
2. Good later: add premium/high-touch tier for serious traders
3. Later expansion: B2B/API licensing for newsletters, communities, or advisors
4. Weak fit now: one-time purchase, because ongoing signals create ongoing cost and expectation

Execution advice:
- Sell outcome + trust, not complexity
- Emphasize transparency: signal history, hit rate methodology, disclaimers, confidence score, sample alerts
- Ask for annual only after monthly retention is proven
- Avoid too many tiers at launch; choice overload will hurt conversion

Simple launch packaging:
- Free: 3 to 5 signals/week, delayed by 15 to 60 minutes
- Pro monthly: real-time signals, more assets, push/Discord/email alerts, better filtering, dashboard history
- Pro annual: 2 months free equivalent
- Founders plan: discounted monthly for first cohort in exchange for feedback/testimonials
