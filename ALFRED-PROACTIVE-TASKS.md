# Alfred Proactive Task Pool

Automated proactive work pool. Rotated via `alfred-proactive-check.sh`. See `AGENTS.md` for idle loop integration.

---

1. **Passive income idea scan**

Scan 3-5 new passive income ideas that fit Joe's expertise (law firm software, automation, developer tools, trading/crypto, SaaS). Validate against:
- Joe's existing skills / projects
- Market fit (TAM, traction, competition)
- Implementation ease (solo vs. team)
- Revenue potential (low/medium/high)

Output: Short report with idea rankings. Document to `reports/passive-income-scan-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

---

2. **Canada-specific passive income scan**

Identify passive income opportunities specific to Canadian freelancers/entrepreneurs:
- Tax-efficient strategies (RRSP, TFSA, corporate structures)
- Canadian SaaS/course platforms
- Regional opportunities (Montreal tech ecosystem, etc.)
- Regulatory advantages/disadvantages

Output: Report to `reports/canada-passive-income-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

---

3. **CoinUsUp growth audit**

Review CoinUsUp analytics, feature usage, user feedback:
- Top features by user count
- Churn signals
- Feature gaps vs. competitors
- Growth opportunities (partnerships, integrations, marketing)

Output: Audit report to `reports/coinusup-audit-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post findings to relevant kanban card.

---

4. **Even Us Up growth audit**

Review Even Us Up analytics, feature usage, user feedback:
- Penetration by use case (roommates, friend groups, couples)
- Monetization potential
- Churn patterns
- Feature requests

Output: Audit report to `reports/even-us-up-audit-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post findings to relevant kanban card.

---

5. **Signal App monetization strategy**

Design a monetization strategy for the Stock/Crypto Buy/Sell Signal App:
- Freemium model options
- B2B partnerships (brokerages, advisors)
- Algorithmic trading / copy-trading integrations
- Premium signal tiers

Output: Strategy document to `reports/signal-app-monetization-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post to kanban Signal App card.

---

6. **Workflow efficiency scan**

Audit Alfred + Joe's workflows for inefficiencies:
- Repetitive manual tasks that could be automated
- Missing integrations (GitHub, Discord, Slack, email)
- Kanban bottlenecks
- System reliability issues

Output: Efficiency report with prioritized improvements to `reports/workflow-efficiency-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post blockers/improvements to kanban.

---

7. **Passive income portfolio review**

Quarterly review of Joe's passive income portfolio (CoinUsUp, Even Us Up, Signal App, other ideas):
- Growth trajectory
- Revenue vs. time investment
- ROI analysis
- Synergies between products
- Recommendation for next 3 months

Output: Portfolio review to `reports/passive-income-portfolio-review-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post recommendations to kanban.

---

8. **Alfred infrastructure improvement scan**

Audit Alfred's infrastructure, automation, and reliability:
- Memory system robustness
- Cron job reliability
- Session/context continuity
- Tool integration gaps
- Performance bottlenecks

Output: Infrastructure audit to `reports/alfred-infrastructure-audit-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Post improvements/fixes as kanban cards or implement autonomously.

---

9. **Alfred ↔ HAL collaborative discussion**

Collaborative discussion between Alfred and HAL on a strategic or operational topic:
- Joe's portfolio focus (where to spend energy next quarter)
- Even Us Up differentiation vs. Splitwise
- Alfred and HAL self-improvement
- New passive income direction validation
- Feature prioritization for any app

Process: Alfred forms perspective, spawns HAL for his take, synthesizes both views into actionable insights. Output structured discussion summary to Discord + kanban.

---

End of proactive task pool.
