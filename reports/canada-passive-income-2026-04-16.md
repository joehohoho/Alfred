### Option B: Federal Corporation (CCPC — Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation)
**Best for:** > $50k/yr passive income; want tax deferral and splitting opportunities

**Tax Treatment (NB + Federal):**
- Corporate tax rate: 13.5% combined (federal + NB small business deduction)
- Personal marginal rate when dividends drawn: 40-50%
- **Total blended rate with dividend:** 25-35% (vs. 40-50% as sole proprietor)
- **Deferral benefit:** Can leave profits in corp, invest, and defer personal tax
- Capital Gains Exemption: $1.016M (2024) lifetime exemption on CCPC shares; can shelter $1M in gains tax-free

**Passive Income Ideas Fit:**
- Legal Tech Templates ($135-180k/yr) — STRONG FIT
- Crypto Signal Paid Tier ($300-600k/yr) — STRONG FIT
- Automation White-Label ($1M+/yr) — STRONG FIT

**Setup Cost:** $500-1,500 (legal/accounting)  
**Annual Cost:** $1,500-2,500 (accounting + compliance)  
**Payoff Threshold:** ~$75k/yr (corporate tax savings offset annual costs)

**Example — Legal Tech Templates at $150k/yr:**
- Sole Proprietor: $150k × 45% = $67,500 tax
- CCPC Structure: 
  - Corp income: $150k × 13.5% = $20,250 tax (corp stays in corp)
  - Retain $129,750 in corp for reinvestment/compounding
  - Draw $50k dividend: $50k × 35% blended = $17,500 tax
  - **Total tax: $20,250 + $17,500 = $37,750 (vs. $67,500)**
  - **Savings: $29,750/yr (44% tax reduction)**

## GST/HST Compliance (Critical for Passive Income)

**Threshold:** $30k revenue/yr = must register for GST/HST

**HST Rate in New Brunswick:** 15% (harmonized)

**How It Works:**
- Charge customers HST (e.g., $100 SaaS → $115 with HST)
- Claim input tax credits on business expenses
- Net effect: Your 15% is passed to CRA; not a business cost

**Digital Services (SaaS):**
- Domestic (Canada): HST applies
- International (US/EU customers): 0% HST (zero-rated for exports)
- **Smart strategy:** Price in CAD (domestic) + USD (international); zero-rate USD sales

**Example — Legal Tech Templates at $150k/yr (70% Canada, 30% US):**
- Canadian revenue: $105k → charge $120.75k with HST → remit $15.75k HST
- US revenue: $45k → 0% HST (export zero-rated)
- Net: $150k revenue, $15.75k HST to CRA; your cost = 0% (passed through)

**Tools:** Stripe/Paddle handle HST calculation automatically; you remit quarterly to CRA.
