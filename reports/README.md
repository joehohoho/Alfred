## Rollback / Pivot Rules

**If an experiment shows <1% conversion / <20% adoption by week 4:** Pivot immediately. Don't throw good time after bad.

**If consulting retainer client is unhappy after month 1:** Pick a different outcome from your list; retry with a new offer design.

**If you can't ship an experiment by target date:** Scope down or extend timeline. Trying to do too much at once will sink everything.

**If a project is taking way more time than planned:** That's data. Update your estimates; adjust priorities.
