---

## Why Productize?

Consulting is your income anchor ($10K–$12K/mo), but it's linear: more clients = more hours. Productization lets you:
1. **Capture value** from repeating work (automation, process, deliverable)
2. **Reduce delivery time** (pre-built templates, scripts, workflows)
3. **Scale horizontally** (retain 1–2 clients, add 5–10 others at lower touch)
4. **Build toward passive income** (retainer replaces project work)

**Reality:** You'll never fully automate consulting (client relationships matter), but you can convert 60–70% of delivery into a repeatable offer.

**Scoring:** For each, rate:

**Examples:**
- "Data Automation Retainer"
- "Compliance & Logging Accelerator"
- "BI Dashboard as-a-Service"

### Scope (What's Included)

**Fixed monthly deliverables:**
- Example: "1 new data source integration + 2 hours tactical support"
- Example: "1 dashboard refresh + 4 hours process optimization"

**Out of scope:**
- Custom development (beyond standard integrations)
- 24/7 support (limit to business hours)

### Pricing

**Formula:** (Delivery hours/month × Hourly rate) + 20% margin

**Example:**
- Delivery: 10 hours/month
- Your rate: $200/hr (consulting rate)
- Cost: $2,000
- Retainer price: $2,500–$3,000/mo (20–50% margin)

**Your estimate:**

- Example: "Database audit, integration planning, initial build"

### Success Metrics (How You'll Prove Value)

## Step 3: Build the Offer (2–3 Weeks)

**Week 1: Document the workflow**
- Write down your current delivery process step-by-step (e.g., "Audit → Design → Build → Deploy → Document")
- Identify reusable pieces: templates, scripts, checklists, configs
- Create a "delivery SOP" (Standard Operating Procedure) so you or someone else could deliver it

**Week 2: Create client-facing assets**
- 1-page pitch (benefit + scope + pricing)
- Onboarding checklist
- Monthly status template
- Sample deliverable (e.g., "What your first integration will look like")

**Week 3: Validate with a pilot client**
- Offer to one existing client: "I'm formalizing a retainer. Try it for 3 months at 20% discount ($2,000 instead of $2,500)."
- Run 3 months; measure: delivery time, client satisfaction, quality of output
- Adjust scope based on actual hours vs. estimate

---

## Step 4: Soft-Launch (Month 2)

Once you've validated the workflow:

1. **Update your positioning:**
   - Website/Slack profile: "Now offering [Offer Name] retainer for [target client type]"
   - Email warm pipeline: 5–10 past clients or prospects who need this

2. **Pricing clarity:**
   - Onboarding: $[X]
   - Monthly: $[Y]
   - Contract: 3-month minimum (gives you predictable time allocation)

3. **Target: 1–2 new clients by end of month 2 (April)**
   - Each at full price ($2.5K–$3K/mo)
   - = $5K–$6K additional MRR

4. **Track results:**
   - How many pitches did you make? (target: 5–10)
   - How many converted? (target: 1–2)
   - What was the close reason? ("Easy integration" vs. "Clear ROI" vs. "Trusted vendor")

---

## Step 5: Systematize & Scale (Month 3+)

Once you have 1–2 clients running the retainer smoothly:

**Option A: Hire delivery help**
- Hire freelancer for 8 hours/month to handle 30–50% of delivery
- You handle relationship + strategic work; they handle integration/admin
- Cost: $1,000–$1,500/mo → keeps $1,500–$2,000/mo profit
- Frees you to sell 2–3 more retainers

**Option B: Productize further**
- Build templated integrations (e.g., 10 pre-built CRM integrations)
- Reduce delivery time 10 hours → 5 hours
- Increase margin or add more clients at same time commitment

**Option C: Add premium tier**
- Standard: $2,500/mo (8 hours/mo)
- Premium: $4,500/mo (15 hours/mo) with 24/5 support + custom integrations
- Target: 3–4 standard, 1 premium = $12K–$14K MRR (20 hours/mo total)

## Red Flags & Adjustments

**Red flag: Delivery takes 2x your estimate**
- Scope is too broad; cut features or increase price
- Or: Pick a different outcome to productize (this one has too many variations)

**Red flag: Client requests constant customization**
- Retainer isn't the right model; offer project work instead
- Or: Re-define "included scope" more strictly

**Red flag: Can't find clients willing to buy**
- Outcome isn't valuable enough; pick a different one
- Or: Pricing too high; validate with 5+ prospects before adjusting down

**Red flag: Pilot takes 20 hours/month but you quoted 10**
- Either: automate 50% of delivery, or
- Increase price to $4,000+/mo to cover hours, or
- Recognize this outcome isn't productizable; pivot

## Why 3-Month Minimum?

A 3-month contract:
- Gives you time to refine delivery + build predictability
- Lets clients see real value (longer ROI timeline)
- Protects your time commitment (billing for at least 3 × $2.5K = $7.5K)
- Sets expectation for renewal + upsell conversations

After 3 months, move to 6 or 12-month agreements (better stability).
