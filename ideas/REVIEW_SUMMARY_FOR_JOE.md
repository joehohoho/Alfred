## Why Now

**Market Gap:** No tool exists that combines:
1. Approval-first workflow (not task board hacked for approvals)
2. Bilingual templates (not English-only)
3. Client portal included (not an add-on)
4. Small-team pricing ($75/mo, not $500+/mo)
5. Atlantic region focus

**Competitive Timing:** Procore is focused on large GCs (high-margin). They can't compete on price/simplicity without cannibalizing their core. We have 6-9 month window before they notice.

**Validation Speed:** Discovery phase (4 weeks) tells us if product-market fit exists. Better to learn now than build for 6 months and find out contractors don't care.
