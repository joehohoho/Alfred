## What We're Building

A **freemium AI-native SaaS platform** that helps nonprofits write grant proposals faster and find aligned funders.

**Core Value Prop:** Instead of spending 40+ hours drafting a proposal manually, nonprofit staff use AI copilot + funder discovery + templates to get from idea to submission-ready proposal in 2-4 hours.

**Monetization:** Free tier (2 proposals/month) + Pro tier ($49-$99/month, unlimited).

**Target Customer:** Mid-market nonprofits ($50k-$500k annual budgets) that need grants but can't afford traditional grant writers ($200-$500/proposal).
