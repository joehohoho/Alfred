## Executive Summary

**Market Gap:** No tool exists that combines:
1. Approval-first workflow (not task management second)
2. Small-team friendliness (not enterprise complexity)
3. Bilingual support (not English-only)
4. Client-facing portal (not internal-only)
5. Atlantic region focus (not global)

**Competitive Position:** We're not competing head-to-head with Procore or Asana. We're creating a new category: "lightweight approval portal for micro-contractor teams."

---

## Competitive Landscape Detailed

### Tier 1: Enterprise Construction Software (Procore, Bridgit, e-Builder)

#### Procore
**Profile:**
- Market leader in construction software
- Serves GCs, architects, owners
- All-in-one: project mgmt, RFQ, change orders, payments, analytics

**Strengths:**
- Mobile-first field management
- Comprehensive (handles entire project lifecycle)
- Integrations (Salesforce, Quickbooks, etc.)
- Strong change order workflow

**Weaknesses:**
- Overkill for 1-20 person teams
- Expensive: $50-200/month minimum, often $1000+/year after setup
- Complex UI (learning curve 2-3 weeks)
- Setup requires consultant (hidden cost $2K+)
- Not bilingual
- Focus on large GC → small contractor is afterthought
- No client-facing portal included (costs extra)

**Why Contractors Don't Use:**
- Budget (small firms pay $100-500/month MAX)
- Complexity (don't need 90% of features)
- Setup pain (want instant, not 3-week onboarding)
- Per-user pricing (teams hate $15/person/mo charges)

**Why We Win:**
- $35-75/month (all-inclusive, no per-user)
- Quote + change order ONLY (not project bloat)
- Bilingual templates included
- 30-min setup (import template, invite team)
- Client portal built-in (free, not add-on)

---

### Tier 2: Project Management Tools (Asana, Monday.com, Notion)

#### Asana
**Profile:**
- General project management
- 100+ integrations
- Large enterprise user base
- Mobile app available

**Strengths:**
- Familiar interface for many teams
- Flexible (customizable workflows)
- Good for timeline tracking
- Integrations ecosystem

**Weaknesses:**
- Approval workflows are clunky (approval is a task, not a document)
- No signature capability (use DocuSign separately = extra cost)
- Client portal weak (read-only, limited)
- Not designed for document approval
- Overkill for contractors (too much feature bloat)
- Per-user pricing ($10-30/person/month, scales badly)
- No bilingual support
- Change order workflows not supported

**Why Contractors Use It:**
- Already have Asana for other projects
- Habit (many teams use it)

**Why They Get Frustrated:**
- Can't route documents for signature
- "Approval" is just marking a task done
- Client view doesn't show version history / changes
- No audit trail for compliance

**Why We Win:**
- Document-centric (quotes and change orders are documents, not tasks)
- Built-in signature + approval workflow (no extra tools)
- Client portal shows actual changes, not just task status
- Bilingual by default
- $75/mo for whole team (not per-user)

---

#### Monday.com
**Profile:**
- Newer, trendier alternative to Asana
- Strong automation
- Visual, drag-and-drop heavy
- Growing market share (SMB favorite)

**Strengths:**
- Beautiful UI (adoption easier)
- Automation powerful (can build custom workflows)
- More developer-friendly than Asana
- Good for task tracking + timelines

**Weaknesses:**
- Still a task board, not document approval system
- Signature not native (integration required)
- Client portal weak
- Per-user pricing ($8-30/person/month)
- No bilingual
- Not built for construction workflows

**Why We Win:**
- Purpose-built for approvals (not trying to jam workflows into task board)
- Signature included (not third-party)
- Client portal is core feature (not afterthought)
- Bilingual templates
- No per-user cost

---

### Tier 3: Construction-Specific Niche Tools

#### Buildr
**Profile:**
- Smaller, newer entrant
- Targets field service contractors
- Photo + crew + schedule focus
- ~$25-50/month positioning

**Strengths:**
- Construction-native
- Affordable
- Mobile-first
- Field crew UX good

**Weaknesses:**
- Not approval-centric (more crew management + scheduling)
- No quote/change order workflow
- No client portal
- No bilingual support
- Still relatively new, limited integrations

**Why We Win:**
- Focused on approvals (their focus is crew scheduling)
- Client portal included
- Bilingual
- Quote + change order workflows

**Differentiation:** We're complementary, not competitive. Buildr + Atlantic Portal could integrate (crew scheduling + approval management).

---

#### Joist (Appointment + Job Management)
**Profile:**
- Service-focused (plumbing, HVAC, electrical)
- Dispatch + scheduling + invoicing
- Mobile app for field crew
- $20-40/month

**Strengths:**
- Very affordable
- Mobile dispatch good
- Integrated invoicing
- Field crew adoption high

**Weaknesses:**
- Not approval-focused
- No change order workflow
- No client portal (or very basic)
- No bilingual support
- Strongly tied to service appointments (less good for renovation projects)

**Why We Win:**
- Purpose-built for approvals (their focus is dispatch + appointments)
- Change order workflows (not their specialty)
- Client portal
- Bilingual
- Works for all trades (not just service appointments)

**Differentiation:** We handle quote/approval friction. Joist handles dispatch friction. Could integrate.

---

### Tier 4: DIY Solutions (Google Forms, Typeform, DocuSign)

#### Manual Combination: Email + Google Forms + DocuSign
**How Contractors Do It:**
1. Google Form collects quote request
2. Manual quote typed up
3. PDF sent via email
4. DocuSign link for signature ($10-50/mo for DocuSign)
5. Manual tracking in spreadsheet

**Strengths:**
- Free/cheap components ($10-50/mo if DocuSign)
- Flexible (build exactly what you want)
- No training needed (tools already known)

**Weaknesses:**
- Time-intensive (manual document + form creation)
- No single source of truth (quotes in email, approvals in DocuSign, tracking in sheets)
- No client portal (customer gets email, confused)
- No approval history
- No change order workflow
- Not bilingual
- Prone to errors (lost approvals, version confusion)

**Why We Win:**
- All-in-one (no piecing together 4 different tools)
- Client portal (client sees project, not scattered emails)
- Bilingual templates (auto-generate EN+FR)
- $75/month saves 20+ hours/month (ROI obvious)
- Audit trail (compliance, disputes)
- Version control (which version did they approve?)

## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths

**Product:**
- Approval-first design (no junk features)
- Bilingual by default (only competitor without this)
- Beautiful client portal (not a checkbox)
- Low-code template system (customization without dev work)

**Market:**
- Underserved segment (Procore ignores small teams)
- Geographic moat (Atlantic-specific)
- High pain point (approvals are broken)
- Sticky workflow (switching costs increase over time)

**Business:**
- Low CAC (word-of-mouth + local partnerships)
- High LTV (contractors pay for years, low churn)
- Low support burden (self-service portal)
- Upsell path (Starter → Professional → Team)

### Weaknesses

**Product:**
- Limited feature set (vs Procore, Asana)
- No mobile app (MVP is web + mobile-responsive)
- No integrations (yet)
- No time-tracking or crew management

**Market:**
- Smaller TAM (just Atlantic Canada, not global)
- Contractor adoption slower (tech resistance)
- Requires sales effort (no "land and expand" like Asana)

**Business:**
- Smaller team can't compete on R&D budget
- Need 3+ pilots to validate (time-consuming)
- Bootstrapped economics (slow growth if self-funded)

### Opportunities

**Market:**
- Expand to other provinces (Quebec, Ontario) post-launch
- Verticals beyond construction (home services, trades, consulting)
- International (bilingual = gateway to Quebec)

**Product:**
- Integrations (QuickBooks, accounting, dispatch)
- Mobile app (native iOS/Android)
- Time tracking + crew management (upsell)
- Field photo documentation
- Subcontractor portal (for GC coordinators)

**Business:**
- Implementation services (templates, customization)
- Training + certification (reseller partners)
- Marketplace (3rd-party templates, add-ons)

### Threats

**Competitive:**
- Procore could launch "SMB lite" product (unlikely near-term, but possible)
- Monday.com could add approval workflows (feature creep → better than us)
- New entrant could copy our idea (bilingual + approval)

**Market:**
- Contractors slow to adopt (tech resistance)
- Economic downturn (reduces construction volume)
- Competitor acquires all our target customers before we scale

**Operational:**
- Pilot recruitment difficult (contractors time-constrained)
- Build delays (scope creep, engineering challenges)
- Codex/HAL availability limits build speed

**Mitigation:**
- Ship fast (MVP in 8 weeks, not 6 months)
- Lock in first 20 customers with high service + testimonials
- Bilingual lock-in (only option for French-Canadian contractors)
- Community + partnerships (chamber of commerce, contractor associations)

## Defensibility & Moat

### Why Competitors Will Struggle to Copy Us

1. **Bilingual is hard** — English UI easy, French + English + legal compliance is 2x work. Competitors delay this.

2. **Local relationships matter** — By the time Procore notices us, we'll have 100+ Atlantic customers who recommend us to friends. Switching costs too high.

3. **Feature simplicity** — Procore can't remove 90% of their product for this market (cannibalizes their core). We can.

4. **Pricing structure** — We make money at $75/mo. Procore needs $500+ to sustain. We win on unit economics.

5. **Time to market** — We'll have customers + case studies by Aug 2026. Any competitor needs 6+ months to launch competing product.

- **Likelihood:** Low (not their market)
