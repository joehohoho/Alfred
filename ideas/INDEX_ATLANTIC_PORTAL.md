## Quick Reference

### Numbers to Remember
- **TAM:** 2,000-2,500 contractors in Atlantic Canada
- **Bilingual TAM:** 300-500 contractors
- **Approval delay baseline:** 7-14 days
- **Target improvement:** 15% = 2-3 days saved
- **Pricing:** $35-75-150/month (starter/pro/team)
- **Willingness-to-pay target:** 70%+ accept $50-150/month
- **Pilot compensation:** $2,000 + free 1-year subscription
- **MVP build timeline:** 8-12 weeks
- **Discovery timeline:** 4 weeks (Mar 25 - Apr 15)

### Go/No-Go Thresholds
- **Approval pain validated:** 8+ calls mention 7-14 day baseline
- **Willingness-to-pay:** 70%+ accept $50-150/month
- **Pilot recruitment:** 3 signed + engaged
- **Market validation:** 70%+ of discovery metrics hit target
- **Bilingual interest:** 50%+ mention French customers

### Risks to Watch
- <30% response rate to outreach (lower interest signal)
- <5 completed calls by week 2 (recruitment issue)
- <50% acceptance on $50-75/month pricing (not viable)
- <2 pilots signed by week 3 (commitment low)
- <30% bilingual demand (moat weaker)
