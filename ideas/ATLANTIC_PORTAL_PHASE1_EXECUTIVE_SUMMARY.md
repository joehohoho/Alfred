## Key Insights From Research

### 1. Approval Cycle Is Real Pain
**Not hypothetical.** Multiple research sources confirm:
- Change orders take 4-12 weeks (vs target 5-7 days with portal)
- Email chains cause 30% of approval rejections (miscommunication)
- Specialty contractors (HVAC, plumbing) have emergency approval needs (same-day)

### 2. Bilingual Is Defensible Moat
**Unique opportunity:** Every major tool (Procore, Asana, Monday) is English-only. New Brunswick is officially bilingual. We can lock 15-25% of market by default.

### 3. Competitor Gap Is Real
**Procore can't compete on price/simplicity:**
- Their $50-200/mo pricing requires enterprise support
- Their 50+ features confuse small teams
- Their enterprise architecture prevents bilingual quick add
- By the time they react, we'll have 100+ customers + switching costs

### 4. Pricing Elasticity High
**Contractors will pay $50-150/month** if time saved is clear:
- Quote approval savings alone (7→2 days) = 5 hours/month
- Change order savings (12 weeks→5 days) = 40+ hours/month
- At $50/hour rate, ROI is obvious

### 5. Design-Partner Pilots Are Essential
**Can't validate without real usage.** Three 4-week pilots will show:
- Actual feature priorities (not what they say they need)
- Bilingual uptake (is 50% using French?)
- Client satisfaction (will they be impressed?)
- Willingness-to-pay (will they renew?)
