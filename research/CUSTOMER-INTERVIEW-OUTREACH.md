## Interview Script (20–30 min)

### Opening (2 min)
"Hi [name], thanks so much for taking the time. I'm researching invoicing and tax compliance for trades operators across Atlantic Canada. I'm exploring building a tool to help with HST and invoice management, and I'd love to hear about your experience. Cool?"

### Section 1: Current Workflow (5 min)

1. **How do you currently invoice your customers?**
   - Spreadsheet, QuickBooks, Wave, handwritten, other?
   - Why that tool?

2. **How many invoices do you issue per month?**
   - Peak vs. slow season volume?

3. **What's the most frustrating part of invoicing today?**
   - Listen for pain: manual entry, HST confusion, tracking paid/unpaid, etc.

4. **Have you ever made a mistake on an invoice?**
   - Wrong HST rate? Missing required fields? Late filing?

### Section 2: Tax & Compliance (8 min)

5. **Do you work in multiple provinces? (NB, NS, NL, PEI)**
   - If yes: How do you handle different HST rates?
   - (Note: NS is 14%, others are 15% — this causes confusion)

6. **Has incorrect HST or late remittance ever cost you money?**
   - Missed ITC deductions? CRA penalty? Interest charges?
   - How much did that impact your business?

7. **How do you keep track of receipts and job costs?**
   - Shoebox? Photos? Spreadsheet? Accounting software?
   - Does it link to invoicing?

8. **How do you remember HST remittance deadlines?**
   - CRA calendar? Calendar reminder? Accountant reminders?
   - Ever missed a deadline? What was the penalty like?

### Section 3: Bilingual & Geographic Specifics (3 min)

9. **Do you serve francophone customers?** (Mostly NB question)
   - Would bilingual invoices (English + French) be valuable?

10. **Are you familiar with NB's bilingual business requirements?**
    - If yes: Does invoicing language ever come up with clients?

### Section 4: Solution & Pricing (4 min)

11. **What would an ideal invoicing tool for trades include?**
    - Features they'd want: receipt capture, job costing, HST automation, deadline tracking, etc.

12. **Automated HST by province** — If you set a client's location (NB vs. NS), the invoice auto-applies 15% vs. 14%. Valuable?

13. **Would you pay $20–60/month for a tool like this?**
    - Test reaction to pricing tiers: $19 (starter) vs. $49 (pro)
    - What would break even for you? (e.g., "saves me 30 min/week" = $300/mo value)

14. **If I built this, would you beta test it?**
    - If yes: take email + details for launch list

### Closing (1 min)
"Thanks so much for this. Really helpful. If I do build this, I'll reach out in a month or two about beta testing. Sound good?"

## Notes for Success

1. **Warm intro > cold outreach:** If Joe knows someone, ask for a warm intro. 50% conversion vs. 10% cold.
2. **Incentivize participation:** $20 gift card or free trial is enough for busy trades operators.
3. **Listen, don't pitch:** Goal is to understand pain, not sell. Resist the urge to demo.
4. **Capture exact quotes:** "He said X was frustrating" is weak. "He said 'I spent 2 hours last month fixing an HST mistake'" is strong.
5. **Follow up:** If someone says "maybe" on beta testing, send a reminder link once MVP is demo-ready.
