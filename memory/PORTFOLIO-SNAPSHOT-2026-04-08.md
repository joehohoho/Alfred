# Portfolio Health Snapshot — 2026-04-08

## Executive Read

Joe's portfolio has one clear near-term revenue lever, one turnaround candidate, one high-upside but not-yet-ready bet, and one dependable cashflow base.

- **Best near-term ROI:** CoinUsUp
- **Best medium-term upside:** Signal App / Market Signal Lab
- **Best turnaround candidate:** Even Us Up
- **Revenue ballast:** Automation Consulting

---

## 1. CoinUsUp

**Current status:** Best near-term passive-income candidate. Core product work is active, and the 14-day free-trial implementation is code-complete but still blocked on Stripe dashboard configuration and approval. Existing research already identified concrete growth levers, especially onboarding, conversion, feature discoverability, and follow-on monetization paths like compliance-style reporting add-ons.

**Estimated current MRR:** Not confidently quantified from the strongest current workspace evidence. Older portfolio notes contain rough numbers, but they conflict, so this snapshot treats current MRR as **unconfirmed** rather than pretending precision.

**Estimated potential MRR, next meaningful milestone:** **+$500 to $2k/mo incremental** if the trial launch is unblocked and followed by onboarding and conversion improvements already identified in workspace research.

**Biggest bottleneck:** Not engineering. The code is already done. The current choke point is **activation and launch completion**, especially the small manual Stripe configuration step plus clearer onboarding and trial conversion flow.

**Why this ranking:** CoinUsUp is the closest project to turning already-built work into additional revenue with the least extra build effort.

---

## 2. Even Us Up

**Current status:** Product exists, but adoption remains weak. Workspace research consistently frames the issue as an activation and clarity problem, not a missing-features problem. The strongest recent audits say the winning path is Canada-first, Interac-native, household and roommate-focused positioning with much sharper onboarding and settlement UX.

**Estimated current MRR:** Effectively **$0 to negligible** from currently trusted evidence. Some older planning notes contain small rough estimates, but the more reliable recent posture is that traction is still too weak to treat it as meaningful recurring revenue.

**Estimated potential MRR, next meaningful milestone:** Roughly **$100 to $300/mo** from an early premium or settlement wedge, but only if activation improves first.

**Biggest bottleneck:** **User activation.** People are not reaching the aha moment fast enough. Research repeatedly points to onboarding, settlement clarity, and first-run guidance as the real blockers.

**Why this ranking:** Even Us Up is salvageable and differentiated, but it needs sharper product storytelling and first-session success before monetization ideas matter.

---

## 3. Signal App / Market Signal Lab

**Current status:** Highest upside of the product bets, but still in internal-quality mode. Workspace analysis is very consistent here: the architecture is solid, the backtesting machinery exists, but signal quality is not yet good enough for confident external commercialization.

**Estimated current MRR:** **$0/mo.**

**Estimated potential MRR, next meaningful milestone:** Near-term commercialization should be framed as **proof of quality, not revenue**. There is meaningful later upside, but the next milestone is objective quality validation, not selling.

**Biggest bottleneck:** **Signal quality and validation.** The project is blocked by a product-readiness problem, not a marketing problem. The best immediate leverage is better diagnostics, reality-checked backtests, and performance scoring.

**Why this ranking:** If quality crosses the threshold, this could become the strongest medium-term upside asset. Right now, it is still too early to push externally.

---

## 4. Automation Consulting

**Current status:** Stable active revenue source and a useful domain-knowledge engine, but not the main passive-income frontier. Workspace guidance repeatedly treats consulting as support and funding, not the core compounding product bet.

**Estimated current MRR:** Active, but **not confidently quantified** from current evidence.

**Estimated potential MRR, next meaningful milestone:** Better understood as **cashflow stability** than passive upside. Its main strategic value is funding product work and exposing reusable pain points.

**Biggest bottleneck:** **Scalability and repeatability.** Consulting remains bespoke, which caps leverage.

**Why this ranking:** Consulting matters, but mostly as ballast and insight generation, not as the primary passive-income engine.

---

## Portfolio Health Read

This is not a weak portfolio. It is an **uneven** one.

- **CoinUsUp** is the closest to turning existing work into near-term revenue.
- **Even Us Up** has product potential, but needs activation repair before expansion.
- **Signal App** has the biggest upside if quality improves enough to justify launch.
- **Consulting** should keep funding the portfolio, not absorb the best product-building cycles.

The common theme is that the bottlenecks are mostly **go-to-market readiness, activation, and validation**, not raw coding capacity.

---

## Recommendation

1. **Unblock CoinUsUp trial launch first.** Then focus immediately on onboarding and conversion improvements.
2. **Treat Even Us Up as an activation problem.** Test one low-effort UX improvement before adding more monetization work.
3. **Keep Signal App in quality-improvement mode** until objective metrics say it is ready.
4. **Use consulting as revenue ballast**, not as the main passive-income bet.
