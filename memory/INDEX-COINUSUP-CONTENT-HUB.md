---

### 2. Platform Choice
**Question:** Where will the content hub live?

**Option A: Ghost** (Recommended)
- Hosting: $25-50/month
- Setup: 4-8 hours
- SEO: Excellent (Ghost native blog)
- Features: Email integration, search, modern design
- **Recommendation:** Easiest + best SEO balance

**Option B: WordPress**
- Hosting: $5-20/month (self-hosted) or $15/month (managed)
- Setup: 8-16 hours
- SEO: Excellent (with plugins like Yoast)
- Features: Unlimited customization
- **For:** If you already use WordPress

**Option C: Substack**
- Hosting: Free or $120/year
- Setup: 30 min
- SEO: Poor (limited authority)
- Features: Email-first, easy publishing
- **Not recommended:** Limited SEO for hub structure

**Decision:** Recommend Ghost on subdomain (tools.coinusup.com)

---

### 3. Resource Allocation
**Question:** Who writes the content?

**Option A: In-House (Your Time)**
- Effort: 8 weeks @ 15-20 hrs/week
- Cost: $0 (your time)
- Quality: You know CoinUsUp best
- **For:** If you have the bandwidth

**Option B: Freelance Writer**
- Effort: 3-4 weeks, your oversight
- Cost: $2,500-4,000 (40-80 hours @ $50-100/hr)
- Quality: Professional writers, you direct
- **Recommended:** Fastest path without your full-time commitment

**Option C: Agency**
- Effort: 3-4 weeks, full-service
- Cost: $8,000-15,000
- Quality: End-to-end service
- **For:** If budget allows and you want hands-off

**Decision:** Option B (freelancer) balances cost/speed
