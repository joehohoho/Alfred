## Content Specifications

### How-To Guides (2,000-2,500 words)
**Structure:**
- Intro (why this matters) — 200 words
- Step-by-step walkthrough — 1,200-1,500 words
- Common mistakes — 300 words
- Tools/template links — 100 words
- CTA to CoinUsUp trial — 50 words

**Examples:**
- "How to Track Nonprofit Donations"
- "How to Track Volunteer Hours"
- "Switching to Nonprofit CRM"

---

### Educational Guides (2,500-3,500 words)
**Structure:**
- Problem statement — 300 words
- Why it matters (impact/cost) — 500 words
- Best practices (5-7 principles) — 1,500 words
- Case study or example — 300 words
- Tools to implement — 300 words
- CTA — 50 words

**Examples:**
- "Nonprofit Donation Management Best Practices"
- "Volunteer Retention Guide"
- "Grant Reporting Best Practices"

---

### Roundup/Comparison Articles (3,000-4,000 words)
**Structure:**
- Intro + selection methodology — 300 words
- Feature comparison table — 500 words
- 8-10 tools reviewed (300-400 words each) — 2,400-3,200 words
- Best-fit recommendations — 300 words
- CTA to CoinUsUp trial — 50 words

**Examples:**
- "Best Free Nonprofit CRM"
- "Free Volunteer Management Software"
- "Nonprofit Donation Tracking Tools Comparison"

## SEO & Conversion Strategy

### On-Page SEO
- **Keyword density:** 1-2% for primary keyword
- **Meta descriptions:** 150-160 chars, include call-to-action
- **H1-H3 structure:** Clear hierarchy, keyword in H1, related keywords in H2s
- **Internal linking:** 5-10 links per article (to other hub content + CoinUsUp pages)
- **CTA placement:** At least 2 above-the-fold, 1 at end

### Conversion Funnel
1. **Top of funnel** — Educational content (grant reporting, best practices)
2. **Middle funnel** — "How-to" guides (CoinUsUp-agnostic, practical value)
3. **Bottom funnel** — CoinUsUp tutorials, comparisons, reviews
4. **CTA strategy** — Soft CTAs early (free templates), harder CTAs (trial signup) for product-specific content

### Internal Linking Map
```
Educational Guide → How-To Guide → CoinUsUp Tutorial → Trial Signup
Educational Guide → Comparison Article → CoinUsUp Review → Trial Signup
Free Template → How-To Guide → CoinUsUp Tutorial → Trial Signup
```

## Content Hub Success Metrics

### Monitoring & Optimization
1. **Keyword rankings** — Track top 20-30 target keywords monthly
2. **Organic traffic** — By content piece, track impressions → clicks → signups
3. **Trial signup source** — Track which content drives most trial signups
4. **Trial-to-paid conversion** — Correlate with content type (educational vs. how-to vs. comparison)
5. **Content performance** — Identify top performers, improve underperformers

### Tools Needed
- **Google Search Console** — Keyword rankings, CTR, impressions
- **Google Analytics 4** — Traffic source, content engagement, conversion tracking
- **Ahrefs/SEMrush** (optional) — Competitor benchmarking, keyword research refinement

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Demand for Specific Keywords
**Mitigation:** Start with high-volume keywords (free volunteer management, nonprofit CRM). Monitor first 4 weeks. Pivot content if needed.

### Risk 2: CoinUsUp Feature Gaps
**Mitigation:** Audit CoinUsUp feature set before content production. Ensure it has donation tracking, volunteer management, grant tracking before promoting.

### Risk 3: Poor Conversion from Organic Traffic
**Mitigation:** A/B test CTAs, landing pages, and trial signup flows. Optimize conversion funnel based on data.

### Risk 4: Content Underperformance
**Mitigation:** Publish 2-3 weeks worth, measure performance, optimize before publishing remaining content.
