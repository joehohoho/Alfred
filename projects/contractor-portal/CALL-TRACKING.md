### Red Flags (if any)

- [ ] Low pain level (avg <6)

- [ ] Consistent pain (7+/10 across calls)
- [ ] Clear product-fit (7+/10 avg)
- [ ] 3+ pilots signed and enthusiastic
- [ ] Repeating feature requests (billing, approval audit trail, mobile)
- [ ] Bilingual opportunity validated

- [ ] Clear referral path to next 10 contractors
