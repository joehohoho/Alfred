## EMAIL TEMPLATE 1: Cold Outreach (Discovery Call)

**Subject:** Quick question about your approval process [HVAC/Renovations/Snow Removal]

---

Hi [Name],

I'm building a simple approval portal specifically for small contractor teams in Atlantic Canada—designed to replace the email/SMS chaos when clients need to sign off on quotes and scope changes.

I noticed your team at [Company] does [Service Type], and I'd love to learn how your current approval process works. Specifically: Do you send out quotes and wait for client approvals via email/text? How many back-and-forths does that usually take?

I'm running discovery calls with 10 contractors to understand the real pain points, and I think your perspective would be really valuable. Would you have 45 minutes sometime next week? No pitch—just a conversation.

**Available times:**
- Tuesday 2-4 PM AST
- Wednesday 10 AM-12 PM AST  
- Thursday 3-5 PM AST

Let me know what works.

Thanks,  
[Your Name]  
[Phone]  
[Simple one-liner about what you're building]

---

**Notes:**
- Keep it short (3 paragraphs max)
- Personalize: mention their service type + specific pain point
- Time options: Show 3 specific windows (easier to say yes)
- No hard sell: "conversation," not "pitch"
- CTA: Direct link to calendar if you have one; otherwise ask for availability

## COMMON OBJECTIONS + RESPONSES

**"I'm too busy right now."**
> "I totally get it. Would it be better to check in a couple weeks from now? I can send you a calendar reminder."

**"We're already using [Procore/Asana/Monday.com]."**
> "Got it. What's one thing you wish [Procore] did better for your approval workflow? I'm trying to understand what's missing." [Listen—this informs product positioning, not a deal-killer.]

**"This sounds like something someone else is probably already doing."**
> "You're right, there are platforms out there. What they usually miss is [bilingual templates / designed for micro-teams / approval-first, not everything-first]. That's the gap I'm going after. But I want to make sure I'm building the right thing, so your feedback matters."

**"I'd need to see it before committing."**
> "That's fair. I'm still in discovery mode right now, so there's nothing to see yet. But once we go through these 10 calls, I'll have a rough prototype in 4 weeks. Would you be interested in seeing it then?"

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "Still figuring that out, but the working idea is $75-150/month depending on features. I want to nail the value prop first. Your feedback helps with that."

## Email Hygiene

- **From address:** Keep it professional (firstname@company.com or simple gmail)
- **Subject lines:** Personalized, benefit-driven (not generic "Quick question")
- **Body:** 3 short paragraphs, not walls of text
- **CTA:** Clear and specific ("45 minutes next week" not "let's chat sometime")
- **Sign-off:** Your name + phone (easy to respond)

---

## Tone Notes

- **Curious, not salesy.** You're learning, not pitching.
- **Specific, not vague.** "Approval process" not "general feedback."
- **Respectful of their time.** "45 minutes" is clear; no open-ended "whenever."
- **Authentic.** Use your own words; don't sound like a template.
