# Discovery Call Framework — Contractor Portal
**Goal:** Validate market assumptions, quantify pain, recruit design-partner pilots  
**Target:** 10 calls, Mar 25 - Apr 11  
**Success Criteria:** 8+ calls completed, 70%+ validation hit rate, 3 pilots signed

---

## Call Structure (45 minutes)

### 1. WARM-UP (5 min)
- Personal intro + why we're building this
- Their background (company size, services, region)
- Why we picked them specifically
- **Goal:** Build rapport, establish credibility

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for making time. I'm building a contractor portal specifically for small teams in Atlantic Canada who manage client approvals through SMS and email chaos. Your background in [HVAC/renovations/snow removal] is exactly why I wanted to talk. Is now still good?"

---

### 2. PAIN DISCOVERY (12 min)
**Open question:** "Walk me through a recent quote-to-approval cycle. What does that look like?"

**Probe questions (listen first, then ask only if needed):**
- How many back-and-forth messages typically?
- Who's involved in approval? (owner, client contact, co-signer?)
- What causes delays most often?
- How do you track approved vs. pending scope changes?
- What happens if a client disputes what was "approved"?
- How do you handle change orders? (same email chaos?)
- What's your current solution? (email, text, spreadsheet, software?)
- If software, what's missing from it?

**Scoring:**
- Pain level: 1-10 (how painful is current process?)
- Frequency: How many quotes/change orders per month?
- Cost of pain: Estimate revenue lost to approval delays, disputes, re-work

**Red flags (note these):**
- Client uses dedicated software (Procore, Asana) — they may not switch
- Process already optimized → lower fit
- Volume too low (<5 quotes/month) → less sticky

---

### 3. SOLUTION EXPLORATION (12 min)
**Intro:** "Based on what you've said, I'm thinking a portal where clients can approve scopes and sign off on changes in one place — no email chains. Would that help?"

**Probe questions:**
- What would be most valuable to you? (approval speed, audit trail, payment visibility?)
- Would your clients use a portal, or would they ignore it?
- How often would you need to send out new quotes/updates?
- Would you need integration with anything? (accounting, CRM, payment?)
- What would make you adopt this vs. stick with email?
- Price sensitivity: What would you expect to pay? ($0-50/month? $50-150?)
- Would you pay more for implementation help (getting clients onboarded)?

**Score:**
- Product-market fit: 1-10
- Willingness to try: 1-10 (would they beta test?)
- Price acceptance: Stated range

---

### 4. DESIGN-PARTNER RECRUITMENT (10 min)
**Transition:** "I'm looking for 3 contractors to pilot this with me — I'll build it custom to your workflow for the first 3 months, at a steep discount [or free], in exchange for weekly feedback. Interested?"

**If yes:**
- Timeline: Start Apr 1, go live in 30-45 days, feedback every week
- Commitment: 30 min/week for feedback calls
- What does success look like for them?
- Who else in their team needs to be involved?
- Schedule first feedback call (tentative)

**If no:**
- Ask why (too risky, too busy, already using something?)
- Ask if they'd be happy to be contacted again in 3 months

---

### 5. CLOSE (4 min)
- Thank you, recap what resonated
- Next step: "I'll follow up in 2 weeks with what we've learned, and I'd love your feedback"
- Ask for 1-2 referrals: "Is there another contractor you'd recommend I talk to?"

## Notes for Caller

- **Energy:** Curiosity-driven, not sales-pitch. You're learning, not selling.
- **Listen more than talk:** 70% them, 30% you.
- **Take notes:** Names, quotes, exact pain points — these become marketing copy later.
- **Follow-up:** Send a 2-line thank-you email same day. Mention 1 thing they said that resonated.
- **Referrals:** Every call should yield at least 1 name for follow-up.
- **Pilot recruiting:** Don't hard-sell pilots. Explain value (custom build, steep discount) and see who bites.

## Go/No-Go Decision Framework (Apr 15)

**GO if:**
- 8+ calls completed
- 70%+ avg pain ≥7
- 70%+ avg product-fit ≥7
- 3 design-partner pilots signed
- Clear pattern in desired features (not all over the map)

**NO-GO if:**
- <8 calls or <70% validation on pain/fit
- <2 pilots willing to commit
- Red flags: market already using dedicated software, low volume, low price acceptance
- Unclear feature set (calls contradictory)

**PIVOT if:**
- Strong product fit but niche is smaller (e.g., only HVAC wins, not all trades)
- Feature set narrows (e.g., approval-first, not payment tracking)
- Pricing must shift (e.g., $20/month instead of $75+)
