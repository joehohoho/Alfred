### 3. **Canadian SMB Compliance Automation — "ComplianceBot"**

**Problem:**

- **Payroll deadlines** (CRA deduction remittance, T4 filing, provincial specifics)
- **Industry-specific rules** (e.g., home care, real estate, food handling)

Cost to hire a compliance consultant: $2,000-5,000/year. But only 20% of SMBs do it.

**Target Market:**
- Canadian SMBs (1-50 employees) across all industries
- SaaS companies operating in Canada (US founders needing Canadian compliance guidance)
- **TAM:** ~2M Canadian SMBs × 20% adoption × $100-200/year = $40-80M market

**Solution:**
Compliance checklist + automation platform:
- Personalized compliance calendar (based on industry + province + company size)
- AI-powered deadline tracking: When is your GST return due? Your payroll deadline?
- Automated form generation (GST/HST worksheets, privacy notices, employee contracts)
- Document templates (industry-specific, legally reviewed)
- Compliance score dashboard: What are you missing?
- Integration with accounting software (QuickBooks, Wave)
- Educational content: What do you actually need to do?

**Why Joe Can Win:**
