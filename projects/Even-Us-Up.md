## 📈 Growth Audit Results (2026-04-13)

### Key Findings

1. **Onboarding Friction** — Setup takes 8-12 min; competitors do 3-5 min
   - Root cause: Settlement explanation unclear; users don't understand what "settle" does
   - Impact: Estimated 20-30% drop-off at settlement step
   - Fix effort: 4-6 hours
   - ROI: +20-30% completion rate

2. **Lack of Referral Program** — No growth loop
   - Current: Only organic (word-of-mouth)
   - Gap: No incentive to invite friends
   - Fix: $5-10 referral rewards (bootstrapped cost: ~$500/month at 50 signups)
   - ROI: Highest (referral is stickiest acquisition)

3. **Missing OCR for Receipts** — Manual expense entry is tedious
   - Current: Type in amounts manually
   - Gap: Can't take photo of receipt → auto-extract amounts
   - Fix effort: 8-12 hours (integrate with Google Vision or similar)
   - ROI: Medium (improves UX but not growth)

4. **Settlement UI Confusion** — Users don't trust the money flow logic
   - Current: Settlement happens automatically
   - Gap: No clear explanation of who pays who what
   - Fix: Add settlement preview + timeline
   - Impact: Estimated +30% trust → higher retention
